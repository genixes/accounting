01 Why it has to fit

Most business software was built for somebody else. A bank in Singapore. A chain in California. A company with its own IT department.

  1. a.

    Too big.

    You pay for two hundred features and use nine. Your staff learns workarounds instead of the work.

  2. b.

    Too foreign.

    Priced in dollars, built around someone else's taxes and habits, and supported from a time zone that is asleep when your shop is open.

  3. c.

    Too expensive.

    Per-seat monthly fees that grow faster than the business does.

So we build the one that fits.
Then we stay to make sure it actually works.

      06 The standard

      S.E.C. Three tests every build has to pass before it ships.

      1. S

        Solution

        It solves the actual problem, not the one a template was built for.

        Test · Can the owner name the step it removed?

      2. E

        Experience

        If staff avoid it, it failed. We design for the busiest hour, the oldest phone, and the newest hire.

        Test · Can a new cashier use it on day one?

      3. C

        Convenience

        It makes the day shorter. No number should ever have to be typed twice.

        Test · Does any figure get entered twice? Then it is not done.

      07 How we build

      From the first call to long after launch.

      1. 01 · Discovery

        We learn how the day runs.

        A free call, then time with the people who will use it. We watch the counter, the front desk, the site office.

        You get · A plain-language problem statement

      2. 02 · Blueprint

        You see it before we build it.

        System Builder maps every screen and every hand-off. You approve the plan, the scope, and the price before a line of code.

        You get · Screens, flows, a fixed scope

      3. 03 · Build

        Short cycles, real devices.

        You test each part on your own phones and tablets while it is being built, not after.

        You get · Working pieces, early

      4. 04 · Launch

        Set up, moved in, trained.

        We bring your existing data across, set up the devices, and train your staff until they stop asking us.

        You get · A team that uses it

      5. 05 · Stay

        We do not disappear.

        Fixes, changes, and new needs as the business grows. Same people, same standard, a message away.

        You get · A builder on your side
