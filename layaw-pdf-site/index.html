Chapter I Edit

Change the words. Keep the look, as close as we can.

Draw a box over a line and Layaw PDF reads what's really there: the actual text, its true point size, and a close match for its typeface.

We'll be straight with you. No PDF editor can perfectly re-flow arbitrary documents in their original embedded fonts, and we don't pretend to. On clean, typed documents the result is excellent. On dense or unusual layouts, you may see a difference. Either way, your edit stays editable: move it, resize it, retype it.

    A signature that notices.

    Separate from a drawn signature stamp, Digital Sign creates a real cryptographic signature (RSA-2048, SHA-256, PKCS#7) over the document's bytes. Change anything afterwards and every reader can tell, Layaw PDF included.

    About that certificate: it's self-signed, so it proves the file hasn't changed since it was signed with that key. It doesn't have a certificate authority vouching for who the signer is. We'd rather you know that than assume otherwise.

    Saved signature

    Your signature, under lock.

    Save your signature once, protected by a password. It's encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived through 210,000 rounds of PBKDF2, and it's unlocked in memory only for the session. There's no back door. If you forget the password, the only way forward is to delete it and start again.

    FAQ

    Straight answers.

    Can it really edit the existing text in my PDF?

    Yes, as a best effort. Draw a box over the text and Layaw PDF detects what's there, its real size and the closest of its 16 font families. The original embedded font's exact letter shapes can't be reproduced, so on dense or unusual layouts the edit may look slightly different. You can always adjust the font, position and size by hand.

    What's the difference between Redact and Whiteout?

    Redact removes the content. The page is rasterized with the box burned in and the original text and graphics are replaced, so nothing is left to recover. That page then becomes a flat image and can't be text-searched.

    Whiteout is an opaque cover that only hides content visually. The content underneath is still in the file. Use it for layout fixes, never for secrets.

    Are the digital signatures trusted by Acrobat?

    A reader like Acrobat will show the document as signed and flag any change made afterwards. The certificate is self-signed, though, so it proves the document is unchanged, not who the signer is. There's no certificate authority behind it.

    Will PDF/A conversion pass a validator?

    We don't promise that. Layaw PDF adds a valid sRGB colour profile and PDF/A-1b metadata and removes encryption. Anything it can't safely fix, like a missing font or transparency, is reported to you as a warning instead of being glossed over.

    How accessible are the PDFs it produces?

    Layaw PDF can tag documents at page level, set the document language and add alt text that screen readers announce. Its Accessibility Checker reports what's really in the saved file. It is not a full PDF/UA authoring tool, and doesn't claim per-paragraph structure.

    Does it need the internet?

    Only for license activation, the first OCR run, optional cloud storage and update checks you ask for. Everything else works offline, and an offline stretch never locks you out.

    I bought a new PC. What happens to my key?

    Open the License dialog on your old PC and choose Deactivate this PC. Then activate the same key on the new one. Reinstalling the app on the same PC doesn't use up your key.
