      The company standard

      S.E.C.

      Every build passes three tests before it reaches your staff. We named them so we can't skip them.

      1. S

        Solution

        Solve the actual problem, not the one a template was built for.

        The testCan we describe your problem back to you, in your words, before we design anything?

      2. E

        Experience

        If staff avoid it, it failed.

        The testDoes the cashier reach for it on a busy Saturday, without being told?

      3. C

        Convenience

        No number should ever be typed twice.

        The testFollow one sale from counter to books. Count the times it is entered. The answer is one.

      How a build runs

      Four steps. The last one never ends.

      1. 01

        Discovery call

        Free, over Messenger. Walk us through a normal day: who does what, where the numbers live, what keeps breaking. No slides, no pressure.

      2. 02

        System Builder scope

        We map your workflow, people and numbers, then agree in writing what gets built, what does not, and what it costs.

      3. 03

        Build in the open

        You see working screens early and often, on the phones, tablets and laptops your team already uses.

      4. 04

        We stay

        Training, adjustments and support after launch. A system is finished when your people use it without thinking.
