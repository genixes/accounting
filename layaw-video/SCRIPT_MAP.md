# LAYAW SYSTEM — Launch Video: Script Map, Storyboard & Notes

**Narration:** `Layaw_System_Marketing_1.mp3` — 39.13 s, mono, 44.1 kHz. Used untouched (no re-record, speed change, or trimming).
**Language:** English (natural conversational Philippine-business tone; a few Taglish/Bisaya words appear only inside the on-screen *mock* chat messages, never in the narration).

> **Transcript confidence.** There is no hosted speech-to-text model reachable from this build environment, so the transcript was produced with an offline recognizer and cross-checked by re-decoding at several playback speeds. Word *timings* are solid (±50 ms). Most *wording* is solid. Four phrases were inferred from sound and context and are marked ⚠. If any is wrong, edit `src/captions.ts` (one line per caption) and re-render. Nothing else in the video depends on them.

## 3. Assumptions & decisions

- **Platform/length:** Facebook/TikTok/Reels/Shorts-first; length = narration length (39.13 s = 1,174 frames @ 30 fps). Not padded or cut.
- **Footage:** no stock library or camera footage is available to this build, and I chose not to fake "real" footage. All visuals are original motion graphics and stylized UI mockups built in code (Remotion/React). The notebook, café POS, court booking and construction Gantt scenes are illustrated, with local (Cebu/PH) cues in the mock data (Café Sugbo, pandesal/ensaymada/ube latte, "Asa na ang resibo?"). If you want real PH footage cut in, the hook (0–6 s) and the "not alone" beat (6.5–8.6 s) are the natural slots.
- **Claims:** only the products named in the narration are shown (POS, Booking, Project Manager, PDF editor). Feature labels come from your company profile. No prices, statistics, testimonials, awards, or guarantees. "Company System" / "Accounting" ("built on request") are *not* shown as downloadable apps; the build-around-you scene uses generic Clients/Quotations/Reports modules to illustrate custom systems.
- **Mock data:** POS shows items and quantities but no peso amounts; the booking calendar's "October 2026 / Saturday 17" is placeholder UI content.
- **Logo:** the supplied PNG is used as-is (no redraw, recolor or distortion), only on white or white tiles, with clear space.
- **Fonts:** Poppins (headlines 700 / support 400), Inter (UI mockups 500/700), Caveat (handwriting in the paper-notebook scenes only). All open-source (SIL OFL).

## 4. Audio

- **Music + sound design are original**: synthesized from scratch in `audio/make_audio.py` (numpy/scipy; no samples, no loops, no third-party music). 120 BPM, C major / A minor. Bar grid is offset so **bar 9 lands exactly on the logo reveal (16.5 s)**.
  - 0–4.5 s minimal ticking pulse + soft pad (curiosity) → 4.5–16 s tension: minor chords, rising arp filter, kick enters at 8.5 s, hats at 10.5 s → 15.55–16.48 s drop-out + riser → **16.5 s** impact, bell sting, full groove (kick, clap, hats, bass, arp, pentatonic hook) → 30.5–33.5 s pulled back, warm bells ("tell us how you work") → 33.65/34.24/34.79 s chord stabs on SIMPLE / SMART / SOLID → **35.4 s** resolved Cadd9 + sting, fade-out from 37.3 s.
  - Sound design: whooshes on every cut, paper rustles, chat pops, keyboard ticks, UI clicks/chimes, impacts on text slams, riser into the logo.
- **Ducking:** music is sidechain-ducked **14 dB** under the narration (25 ms attack / 380 ms release) and breathes back up in pauses. Music averages ≈ 20 dB under the narration overall.
- **Master:** narration + music + SFX → limiter → two-pass loudness normalization: **-13.9 LUFS integrated, -1.5 dBTP true peak**, no clipping.
