SPECIALTY MODULES
1. Growth Charts: WHO/CDC percentile curves for weight, height/length, head
   circumference, BMI; plot measurements over time; flag under/overweight.
2. Immunization Tracker: vaccine schedule (BCG, Hep B, Pentavalent, OPV, PCV,
   MMR, Flu, etc.), due/overdue/completed status, lot numbers, printable
   vaccination card, reminder campaign to parents.
3. Family Account: parent/guardian linked to multiple children, one login,
   switch child profile, authorized pickup persons.
4. Developmental Milestones Checklist by age (motor, language, social) with
   concern flags.
5. Weight-based Dose Calculator: auto-compute pediatric dosing from weight with
   max-dose safety warnings.
6. Sick vs. Well-baby Visit Templates: newborn screening, feeding history,
   sleep, rashes, fever diary.
7. Parent Portal: book visits, download vaccine card, growth chart, school
   medical certificate request, chat with clinic.
8. Waiting Room Display: kid-friendly queue screen with avatars/nicknames.

EDITION DATA MODEL: Guardian, Child, GrowthMeasurement, VaccineSchedule,
VaccineRecord, MilestoneAssessment, DoseCalculation, SchoolCertificate.

DASHBOARD KPIs: vaccine coverage %, overdue vaccines list, new vs. returning
patients, top diagnoses (URTI, gastroenteritis), average visit duration,
recall conversion.
