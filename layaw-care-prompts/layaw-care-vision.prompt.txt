SPECIALTY MODULES
1. Eye Exam Record: visual acuity (OD/OS), refraction (sphere, cylinder, axis,
   add, PD), IOP, slit-lamp findings, fundus notes, interactive eye diagram.
2. Prescription Generator: eyeglass and contact lens Rx, printable with
   letterhead.
3. Optical Retail POS: frames catalog with images, lens types (single vision,
   progressive, blue-cut), coatings, price calculator, lab order, pickup
   status (Ordered, Fabricating, Ready, Claimed).
4. Surgery Scheduler: cataract/LASIK/pterygium cases, OR booking, pre-op
   checklist, post-op follow-up schedule.
5. Diagnostics Imaging: OCT, visual field, fundus photo gallery with compare.
6. Optical Inventory: frames and lenses by SKU, size/color variants, low-stock
   alerts, supplier orders.
7. Recall: annual exam and lens-replacement reminders.

EDITION DATA MODEL: EyeExam, Refraction, ContactLensRx, GlassesRx, Frame,
LensType, OpticalOrder, SurgeryCase, DiagnosticImage.

DASHBOARD KPIs: exams per day, optical sales, exam-to-purchase conversion,
surgery pipeline, frame inventory turnover, top lens brands.
